We stand because they stood

Ndaa! Before this child had a name, there were grandmothers who sang at dawn and grandfathers who kept watch over the village.

This is our Musumo wa Nwana: a day to welcome our child with song, drum and prayer. We call on Midzimu, the ancestors who walked before us, to bless this little one and the road ahead.

Come with your voice, your drums and your family’s pride, and honour them with us.

Our Venda heritage

Tshikona

The reed-pipe dance of the Vhavenda. Every pipe carries a single note, and together they make one song, danced to honour chiefs and ancestors.

Ngoma

The drum that opens our gatherings and carries our prayers to Midzimu.

Makhadzi

The father’s sister, who holds a place of honour in Venda family ceremonies and blessings.
